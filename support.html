Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the direction arrow spin or point the wrong way?
Stationary phone compasses can be affected by magnetic interference inside metal structures or concrete garages. BackTrack includes an AI Motion-Aware Trajectory Engine: take 5 to 10 walking steps, and the algorithm detects your physical walking direction and pivots the arrow directly to match your heading.
How do I restore my BackTrack Pro subscription?
If you upgraded your device or reinstalled the app, open the subscription paywall modal and tap "Restore Purchases". BackTrack will securely query Apple StoreKit 2 and verify your receipt instantly without charging you again.
How does the 3-day free trial work?
All new users receive 3 full days of unlimited Pro features (3D AR HUD, AI Sign OCR, unlimited GPS breadcrumb trails, and fee ticker alarms). You can cancel at any time in your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions before the trial ends without being charged.
Does BackTrack work underground without cellular service?
Yes! BackTrack is built offline-first. GPS satellite signals, digital compasses, camera AR overlays, and garage voice notes operate completely without cellular data or Wi-Fi.
How do I log my spot in an underground multi-level parking garage?
When you park, tap "Park Here Now". In the parking sheet, tap your floor number (e.g. B2, P4), pillar letter/number (e.g. C-14), and tap the microphone icon to record a quick 1-tap voice landmark memo right by the elevator.
